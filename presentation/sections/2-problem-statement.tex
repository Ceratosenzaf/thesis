\subsection[ ]{What's happening in Rust?}\label{subsec:rust_issue}

\begin{frame}{\secname}
	\framesubtitle{\subsecname}

	In 2015, an RFC introduced specialization to Rust as an unstable feature confined to the \alert{Nightly Channel}. % Rust's unstable channel, where new features are tested before being stabilized.

	\bigskip

	However, due to unresolved design issues and concerns about long-term maintenance, specialization is still unstable.
	% This has led to frustration within the Rust community, as many developers were eager to leverage specialization for performance optimizations.

	\bigskip

	Without stable specialization, developers often resort to code duplication or complex workarounds.
	% This leads to increased maintenance costs and potential for bugs, other than performance degradation and missed optimization opportunities.

\end{frame}
